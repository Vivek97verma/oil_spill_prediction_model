# oil_spill_prediction_model
Developed an advanced Oil Spill Prediction Model aimed at forecasting potential oil spill incidents using various predictive features. The model integrates historical data and environmental variables to provide timely predictions and risk assessments, which are crucial for preventive measures and response strategies in the oil and gas industry.

Features:

Historical Spill Data: Records of past oil spills, including location, volume, and impact.
Environmental Variables: Weather conditions (e.g., wind speed, temperature), ocean currents, and water salinity.
Operational Factors: Oil drilling activities, transportation routes, and equipment status.
Geographical Information: Maps and data related to vulnerable ecosystems and high-risk areas.

Tools Used:

Python: For data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model building using libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-Learn.
Machine Learning Algorithms: Implemented classification and regression techniques to predict spill occurrences and their potential impact.
Jupyter Notebook: For development and documentation of the model.
Matplotlib & Seaborn: For data visualization and analysis.

Key Achievements:

Developed a robust model that enhances predictive accuracy for oil spill incidents.
Implemented data-driven insights to aid in risk management and mitigation strategies.
Improved response times and preparedness through accurate predictions.
